The challenge

How could a local historical site attract more visitors while keeping its programming connected to its history? In fall 2023, our Introduction to Business class at Bergen Community College developed tourism proposals under the simulated consulting company Bergecco-Parc Consulting Inc.

The assignment focused on the Bergen County Historical Society at New Bridge Landing in River Edge, New Jersey. Teams were asked to combine event concepts, cost planning, advertising, and fundraising into a presentation.

My part in the team

I participated as a member of Team Two. The work brought together research, marketing ideas, revenue considerations, and recommendations for the historical site. The presentation and competition recognition belong to the team.

What the proposal needed to address

  • Halloween programming: develop a family event using the grounds and historical buildings, informed by traditions from 1785–1900.
  • Spring/Easter programming: propose a welcoming, secular seasonal event grounded in the same historical period.
  • Museum fundraising: plan a 2024 initiative with a $25,000 goal, including costs and promotion.
  • Tourism outreach: consider local residents and visitors from farther afield, with presentation slides and commercials required in English and Spanish.

These were the assignment requirements. Event budgets needed to be developed from the proposed activities; the simulated consulting retainer was separate from the event budget.

What this work connects

The project connected a tourism objective with the practical choices behind it: whom to reach, what an event would require, how to price its inputs, and how promotion could support attendance and fundraising. It also required coordinating ideas into a shared presentation.
