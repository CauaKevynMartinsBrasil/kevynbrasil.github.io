About me

I’m studying finance at Rutgers Business School in Newark. My experience in operations, small-business financing, and legal research has made me interested in both the numbers behind a business and the people making its decisions.

My background

At Master Pizza, I reconcile cash and deposits and examine delivery commissions, payroll, pricing, and marketing spend. Working through these details helps me understand how everyday operating decisions affect a business’s performance.

At High Rise Capital, I identified small-business owners’ funding needs and explained financing options in plain language. In my legal internship, I researched active matters and organized findings for attorneys. Across these roles, I’ve practiced checking details, synthesizing information, and explaining it clearly.

Academic foundation

Education

I’m pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Finance at Rutgers Business School, with an expected graduation in May 2028. I earned an Associate in Science in Business Administration from Bergen Community College in May 2026, graduating with a 3.89 GPA and Dean’s List recognition.

Through accounting, statistics, economics, Python, and spreadsheet work, I’m learning to connect classroom concepts to business questions. I received the Moses Family Endowed Scholarship and the LEO Foundation Endowed Scholarship at Bergen.

Community

Leadership & community

As president of Bergen’s Legal Studies Club, I organized programming and member engagement around law, business, and professional development. At Rutgers, I’m a member of the Association of Latino Professionals For America (ALPFA).

At Bergen, I served as a judicial member of the Student Government Association and participated in the Latin American Student Association and Motion: Student Achievement Club. I speak Portuguese natively and am proficient in Spanish—skills that help me connect with people across backgrounds.
