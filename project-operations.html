Cash Reconciliation & Operations

Cash controls, operating expense analysis, and team coordination at Master Pizza.

The setting

At Master Pizza, I work across operations and team leadership in a location that processes more than 200 orders on a typical day. The pace makes accuracy matter: cash totals, deposits, staffing, and customer service all need attention at once.

My work

Part of my role involves reconciling around $1,000–$3,000 in daily cash and deposits, checking end-of-day totals, and maintaining records. I also examine delivery commissions, payroll, marketing budgets, and pricing to understand the factors that affect margins and operating performance.

  • Check that recorded cash and deposits agree with end-of-day activity.
  • Look at operating costs alongside the volume and pace of the location.
  • Coordinate counter and delivery staff during peak shifts.
  • Train 4–6 new employees on POS systems, order processing, and front-of-house procedures.

Financial relevance

Cash reconciliation supports the reliability of daily records by comparing reported activity with cash and deposits. Delivery commissions and payroll affect the cost of serving orders, while pricing and marketing spend influence how revenue translates into operating performance.

My role provides hands-on exposure to these relationships through daily records and operating data. This experience has strengthened my interest in cost analysis and corporate finance.

Team coordination

Supervising counter and delivery staff connects financial processes to the pace of the business. Training new employees on consistent POS and order-handling procedures supports order accuracy and dependable records during peak shifts.
