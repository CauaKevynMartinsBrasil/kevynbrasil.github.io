Cash Reconciliation & Operations

Cash controls, operating expense analysis, and team coordination at Master Pizza.

On this pageThe settingMy workFinancial relevance

The setting

At Master Pizza, I reconcile cash, maintain deposit records, and review operating costs. I also help coordinate shifts and train staff.

The location handles 200+ orders a day, providing context for the daily records and shift activity.

My work

I reconcile around $1,000–$3,000 in daily cash and deposits, verify end-of-day totals, and maintain deposit records. I analyze delivery commissions, payroll, marketing budgets, and pricing to understand their effect on margins and operating performance.

  • Verify recorded cash and deposits against end-of-day activity.
  • Analyze delivery commissions, payroll, pricing, and marketing spend.

Financial relevance

Cash reconciliation supports the reliability of daily records by comparing reported activity with cash and deposits. Delivery commissions and payroll affect the cost of serving orders, while pricing and marketing spend influence how revenue translates into operating performance.

Reviewing daily records and operating data helps me connect these costs to business performance and strengthens my interest in cost analysis and corporate finance.
